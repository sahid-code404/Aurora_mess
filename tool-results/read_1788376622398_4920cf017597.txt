   647→# 19. FINANCIAL LEDGER
   648→
   649→Implement an immutable double-entry ledger internally.
   650→
   651→Residents do NOT need to see accounting terminology.
   652→
   653→Backend should maintain:
   654→
   655→```text
   656→ledger_accounts
   657→ledger_journals
   658→ledger_entries
   659→```
   660→
   661→A journal contains one business transaction.
   662→
   663→Entries must balance.
   664→
   665→Conceptually:
   666→
   667→```text
   668→SUM(debits) = SUM(credits)
   669→```
   670→
   671→Posted journals cannot be edited.
   672→
   673→A correction creates another journal.
   674→
   675→Examples:
   676→
   677→Payment:
   678→
   679→```text
   680→Dr Cash/Bank
   681→Cr Resident Balance / Receivable
   682→```
   683→
   684→Expense:
   685→
   686→```text
   687→Dr Expense
   688→Cr Cash/Payable
   689→```
   690→
   691→Refund:
   692→
   693→```text
   694→Dr Resident Balance Liability
   695→Cr Cash/Bank
   696→```
   697→
   698→Exact chart-of-account design should be documented.
